BPS School Preview Times
All BPS schools hold information sessions between November and January, where prospective families can tour school facilities, see student work, meet school leaders and staff, and ask questions about their various programs. Schools offer a combination of virtual and in-person information sessions. More information at the preview dates and school information [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-Ae4o7_p_SIU-6wL5tjVgffKRODnnr02/view.
Please email welcomeservices@bostonpublicschools.org with any questions.
