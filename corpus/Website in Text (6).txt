Welcome Centers Locations [https://www.bostonpublicschools.org/enrollment/welcome-services/welcome-centers-locations]
If you are registering your child for the school year starting in September 2025, registration for students entering K0, K1, K2, Grade 6, Grade 7, and Grade 9 will open on January 6. 
Visit bostonpublicschools.org/register for more information and the list of required documents [https://www.bostonpublicschools.org/fs/pages/2737/#RequiredDocuments].
Get a head start on the registration process by pre-registering online! Pre-registration for the 2025-2026 school year is now available. Please visit bostonpublicschools.org/pre-register to pre-register and schedule an appointment at bostonpublicschools.org/appointment.
